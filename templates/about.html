About College Internship Management System

A simple platform for students to find and manage internship opportunities.

🎓 About the System

College Internship Management System is a web-based platform designed to help students find and apply for internship opportunities.

🔍 Find Internships

Students can explore available internships, view company details, required skills, duration and stipend information.

📝 Online Application

Students can apply for suitable internships through the online application system.

📊 Application Tracking

Students can track their applications and check whether their application is Pending, Approved or Rejected.

👨‍💼 Admin Management

Admin can add internship opportunities, edit internship details and review student applications.
